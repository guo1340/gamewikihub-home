Use this page to report incorrect wiki information, request removals, suggest a new wiki, or ask questions about GameWikiHub.

Email

contact@gamewikihub.com

What to include

  • Which wiki and which page or entry you are referring to.
  • What information is wrong, missing, or outdated.
  • A source, screenshot, patch note, or clear explanation when available.
  • For new-wiki suggestions: the game name, where it lives (Steam, itch.io, etc.), and why a wiki would help.

Removal and copyright requests

If you are a rights holder and believe content on a GameWikiHub wiki infringes on your rights, email the address above with the page URL, the specific content in question, and a description of the issue. We will review and respond.

Unofficial site notice

GameWikiHub is an unofficial fan project. For official support, purchasing issues, bug reports, or account problems with a game itself, contact the official game developer or platform support channel - we cannot help with those.
