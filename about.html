About GameWikiHub

Home / About

GameWikiHub is a small, independent network of unofficial fan-made wikis for indie and cult games. The goal is simple: build clean, fast, player-focused references for games that don't already have a good modern wiki - and keep them free of the clutter, pop-ups, and auto-playing ads that plague larger wiki platforms.

What's hosted here

Each wiki lives on its own subdomain (for example, mewgenics.gamewikihub.com) and is structured around the things players actually look up: stats, characters, items, abilities, encounters, locations, and strategy. New wikis are added when a game is interesting enough to deserve one and there isn't already a high-quality reference for it.

Unofficial fan project

GameWikiHub is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the creators, publishers, or rights holders of any game covered here. All game names, characters, art references, and trademarks are property of their respective owners and are used here for informational and identification purposes only.

Content accuracy

Game information changes across updates. Patch changes, balance updates, and community discoveries can make older pages incomplete or wrong. If you spot something out of date or incorrect, use the Contact page to report a correction.

Advertising

GameWikiHub displays advertising to help cover hosting, domain, maintenance, and content-production costs. Ads are placed in a way that should not block or disrupt core wiki content.

Suggesting a new wiki

Got a small or weird game that deserves a clean reference? Suggest it through the Contact page. Suggestions are most likely to be picked up if the game has an active player base, no good existing wiki, and mechanics worth documenting.
