What is GameWikiHub?

GameWikiHub is a small, independent network of fan-made wikis for games that don't already have a clean, modern reference. Each wiki is a static site focused on the things players actually look up — stats, builds, encounter notes, and patch changes — without the clutter, pop-ups, and autoplay videos that plague larger wiki platforms. None of the wikis hosted here are affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the games' developers or publishers.
