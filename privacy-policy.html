Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GameWikiHub and its hosted fan wikis handle basic information when you visit the site.

Information we collect

GameWikiHub is a static website network and does not require user accounts. We do not intentionally collect names, passwords, payment information, or private account details from visitors.

Basic technical information may be processed automatically by hosting, security, analytics, and advertising providers. This can include IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages, approximate location, and timestamps.

Cookies and local storage

The site may use browser storage such as localStorage to remember interface preferences (for example, table filters or theme settings on individual wikis). Third-party services such as analytics or advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand site traffic, popular pages, search behavior, and technical issues. Analytics data is used to improve the site and prioritize wiki content.

Advertising

GameWikiHub uses Google AdSense and may use other advertising providers. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and personalize or limit advertising depending on user settings and applicable law.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services by visiting Google's privacy and advertising documentation.

Third-party links

GameWikiHub and its wikis may link to external websites, stores, social platforms, developer posts, or community resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Children's privacy

This site is intended as a general game-information resource. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the site grows, especially when new analytics, advertising, contact, or community features are added. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact page.
